What LB Seller does

Listing X-Ray (AI audit)

Scores each listing — title, bullet points, A+ content, images, reviews and price — and gives a clear, prioritized list of improvements.

Listing optimization

AI-suggested titles, bullet points and descriptions that the seller reviews and approves before anything is published to Amazon.

Pricing

Monitor your prices and competitive position, see real profit after Amazon fees, and update prices from one screen.

Inventory & FBA

Know what is running out, what is not selling and what is building storage fees. Plan and track FBA shipments.

Sales & profit dashboard

Orders, revenue, fees and margin per product, per day and per marketplace (US, CA, MX).

Monthly performance report

A clear monthly report of sales, account health and the work done on the store.

How it works

  1. Create your account on LB Seller.
  2. Authorize LB Seller in Seller Central through Amazon's official authorization flow (Selling Partner API). You can revoke access at any time.
  3. See your store in one dashboard with the Listing X-Ray, inventory alerts and profit view.
  4. Approve the improvements you want. LB Seller only changes listings or prices when the seller approves.

Data & Security

LB Seller connects to Amazon only through the official Selling Partner API, with the seller's explicit authorization.

  • We use Amazon data only to provide the features the seller signed up for. We never sell or share it with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not request Amazon customers' personal information (names, addresses, phone numbers).
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, access is limited to authorized staff with multi-factor authentication, and credentials are never stored in code.
  • Sellers can disconnect LB Seller in Seller Central at any time and request deletion of their data.
