Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how LB Seller, operated by LB Marketplace (LINCOLN HENRIQUE FREITAS DE SOUZA, CNPJ 58.943.847/0001-90, Av. Pauliceia, 1009, Sala 14, Caieiras, SP, Brazil), collects and uses information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email and company name of the seller who creates an LB Seller account.
  • Amazon seller data: after the seller authorizes LB Seller in Seller Central, we access, through the Amazon Selling Partner API, the seller's listings, prices, inventory, FBA shipments, order totals, fees, financial reports and account performance.
  • We do not request or store Amazon customers' personally identifiable information (such as buyer names, addresses or phone numbers).

2. How we use information

Only to provide the LB Seller features the seller uses: dashboards, listing audits, AI-suggested listing content, pricing and inventory tools, and reports. We never sell seller data and never use Amazon data for purposes other than providing the service to that seller.

3. Sharing

We do not share Amazon information with third parties, except service providers that host and process data on our behalf under confidentiality and security obligations: cloud hosting (Google Cloud / Firebase, Vercel) and AI processing of listing content (Anthropic, Google). Listing content sent for AI analysis does not include buyer information.

4. Security

  • Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access restricted to authorized staff based on job function, with multi-factor authentication.
  • Credentials and API tokens are stored in protected secret storage, never in source code or public repositories.
  • Security incidents involving Amazon information are reported to Amazon (security@amazon.com) within 24 hours of detection.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data only while the seller's account is active and as needed to provide the service. A seller can revoke LB Seller's access in Seller Central at any time and request deletion of their data by email; we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights

Sellers can request access, correction or deletion of their information at any time by contacting us. Residents of California and other U.S. states may exercise the rights granted by applicable privacy laws through the same contact.
